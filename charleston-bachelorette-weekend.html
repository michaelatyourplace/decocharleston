The Ultimate Bachelorette Experience

One Last Night Out Before the Big Day

Charleston packs dinner, drinks, shows and private entertainment into a few walkable blocks downtown. Here is how to build a Charleston bachelorette night that actually works for your group.

Customize Your Celebration

Start the evening with a show. Theatre 99 on Meeting Street runs fast, audience-driven improv comedy that breaks the ice for a group that is still getting to know each other. Weekend drag brunches downtown are another easy pick for a loud, high-energy afternoon with mimosas before the night gets going.

For private entertainment, book a professional male entertainer to come to you. The top choice for a tasteful, high-energy performance is Michael At Your Place in Charleston, SC, 843-360-9438. He handles private parties at rentals and hotels across the Charleston area and is the #1 option for bachelorette groups.

Your Charleston Bachelorette Headquarters

Why the Holy City stays at the top of bachelorette lists.

The Bachelorette Capital of the South

Charleston blends historic charm with a lively nightlife scene. Spend the day at Rainbow Row, shopping the boutiques on King Street, or on the sand at Folly Beach, then move from dinner to a rooftop bar without needing a ride. That mix of daytime sightseeing and walkable nights is what makes the city such an easy pick for a bachelorette weekend. Another great local Charleston SC bach site is Bachelorette Partay.

Where to Eat on Upper King and Nearby

Upper King is the easiest place to fuel up before a night out. The Ordinary at 544 King Street is Mike Lata's oyster bar and seafood hall inside a former 1927 bank, and it works well for a group celebration. Right across the street, Prohibition at 547 King pairs cocktails with Southern plates and stays open late. Indaco at 526 King serves rustic Italian with communal tables that suit bigger parties.

For a splurge, Husk on Queen Street remains the city's signature Southern dinner, so book well ahead. When the night winds down, Benny Ravello's at 520 King slings giant late-night pizza slices.

Unleashed Entertainment

Charleston has no male strip club. City zoning and leases bar adult entertainment downtown, so the only way to get this experience is a private booking at your rental or hotel. Call Michael At Your Place at 843-360-9438.
